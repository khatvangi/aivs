# QSS submission guidelines (extracted from MIT Press, June 2026)

Source: https://direct.mit.edu/qss/pages/submission-guidelines (403 to fetcher; via search snippets)

## Article types and length

- **Articles** (original theoretical, empirical, methodological): **5,000–8,000 words**
- **Reviews** (novel synthesis): 5,000–10,000 words
- **Letters**: up to 1,000 words
- **Book reviews**: 1,000–2,000 words

Our paper is a research Article, methodological + worked case study. Target the 5,000–8,000 band.

## Abstract

- **Limit: 200 words.**

## Keywords

- **Up to 6.**

## Cover letter

- **Required at first submission.**
- **Limit: 300 words.**
- Comment on the significance of the work and its fit with the journal scope.

## Title page / byline

- Names and affiliations of all authors on the first page.
- ORCIDs of all authors must be provided.
- Pages, sections, and subsections numbered.
- Line numbering suggested.

## File format

- Single Word **or** PDF file at first submission.
- Figures, tables, and references all integrated into the one file.
- No strict formatting requirement at first submission; at revision references become APA-style.

## Reference style

- **Author–year format. Numbered references NOT permitted.**
- At revision, conformed to **APA style**.

## Data availability

- **Required statement** explaining how/where data are shared.
- If data cannot be shared, the statement must explain why.

## Code availability

- Sharing of code is **strongly recommended** (not mandatory).

## Peer review

- The journal participates in **transparent peer review** (reviewer reports and author responses published alongside the article when authors opt in).

## Open access

- **Open-access** journal (joint MIT Press / ISSI).
- APC: check Editorial Manager portal at submission; ISSI member / OA institutional waivers exist.

## Generative-AI disclosure

- No QSS-specific GenAI policy was found in our search; ICMJE / MIT Press defaults apply. Practically that means:
  - No AI tool as an author.
  - Disclose any AI-assisted writing and what it was used for.
- This is an opportunity, not a constraint, for our paper: the AIVS bundle itself **is** the AI-use disclosure.

## Submission portal

- Editorial Manager (MIT Press standard).

## Journal scope (for cover-letter framing)

QSS = official open-access journal of the **International Society for Scientometrics and Informetrics (ISSI)**. Publishes theoretical and empirical research on:
- science and the scientific workforce
- the system of science / general laws of scientific work
- **scholarly communication**
- science indicators
- science policy

Our manuscript fit: methodological contribution to scholarly communication infrastructure (a documentation schema for AI-assisted research) plus an empirical worked case study demonstrating it on a complete research workflow.
