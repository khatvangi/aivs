# legacy/

Superseded manuscript material from earlier submission targets (*Patterns*,
then QSS). Retained for provenance only. **None of it is the current paper.**

The current manuscript is the IEEE *Computing in Science & Engineering*
submission CiSE-2026-06-0105, `../var_manuscript_cise.md`. Where this
directory and the CiSE manuscript disagree, the CiSE manuscript is correct.

These files still contain the compound term "AIVS-VAR", which was retired
during the CiSE revision: AIVS is the schema, VAR names the three properties,
and there is no combined method by that name. They are left unedited because
they are a dated record of what earlier drafts argued.

The two `cover_letter_patterns.*` files are the cover letter for the original
*Patterns* submission, addressed to that journal's editor. They carry no
reviewer material — they predate review. The current CiSE revision cover
letter is deliberately untracked; see the 2026-09-19 entry in `../HISTORY.md`.
