## Word limit

- **Feature article: 2,400–6,250 words inclusive of main body, abstract, keywords, bibliography, and biography text.**
- **Each figure and each table counts as 250 words** toward the limit (Software Engineering track guidance; treat as authoritative until confirmed at submission portal).
- Implication for us: with 6 figures + 3 tables = 9 × 250 = **2,250 words of figure/table credit**, leaving **~4,000 words of prose body + refs + bio** under the cap.

## Reference style

- IEEE numbered format `[1], [2], ...` — **matches our current** `[1]–[28]`. No change required.
- Verify journal/conference formatting conforms to IEEE style at revision.

## Abstract / keywords

- Abstract length: not specified explicitly in extracted content; IEEE magazine convention is ~150–250 words. Treat 200-word target as safe.
- Keywords: standard IEEE convention is ~5 index terms.

## Figure / table

- **Minimum 300 dpi at intended display size.**
- TIFF, EPS, or PDF preferred for line art. PNG acceptable for raster.
- Color permitted but production may default to grayscale; design for both.
- Inline at first reference.

## Author block / ORCID / biography

- **ORCID required for every author** (ScholarOne enforces at account creation).
- Author biographies in structured format: role, institution, research interests, degree, professional affiliation, contact.
- Photographs of authors usually requested at acceptance, not submission.

## Submission portal

- **ScholarOne Manuscripts** (Manuscript Central).
- IEEE Author Center handles AI-disclosure boilerplate.

## Data / code availability

- Not extracted verbatim from CiSE page; IEEE-wide policy is a Data Availability Statement (mandatory or recommended depending on track) referencing repository + DOI. Our Zenodo deposits cover this.
- Reproducible-research declarations are explicitly encouraged in CiSE (the journal runs a Reproducible Research Track).

## Competing interests

- IEEE COI form during submission (declared via ScholarOne form, not as a manuscript section). A one-line in-manuscript statement is also common.

## AI / generative-AI disclosure

- Follow IEEE Author Center policy: AI tools may not be listed as authors; use must be disclosed.
- Our manuscript's substance *is* the disclosure mechanism, so the standard IEEE statement plus a pointer to the AIVS bundle is the right pattern.

## Cover letter

- Customary, addressed to Editor-in-Chief (currently Lorena Barba). Our letter is ~280 words — within limits.

## Article types (CiSE)

- Feature articles (2,400–6,250 words)
- Departments (e.g., "Education", "Visualization Corner") — shorter, by invitation
- Theme issues (call-for-papers driven, separate limits)
- Reproducible Research Track (CFP-driven, encourages full RR badge)
